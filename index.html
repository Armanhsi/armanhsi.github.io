Projects

Gaze × physiology → risk
Under review · CHI 2027Dissertation core

RIDER: real-time multimodal risk detection for cyclists

RIDER fuses 120 Hz eye tracking with physiological signals (EDA, HR/HRV) in transformer-based sequence models that predict safety-critical rider states before a collision happens. It's the core of my dissertation on predicting cyclist risk behavior in immersive VR.
